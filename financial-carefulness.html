Student Money Skills

Use your money thoughtfully and build good habits early.

Financial care is about awareness, not having a large income. Track what comes in, plan what goes out, and pause before spending.

Simple habits for financial confidence

🧾

Track Spending

Write down purchases for one month. Seeing small expenses clearly makes it easier to decide what matters.

📊

Make a Simple Budget

Separate needs, study costs, savings, and optional spending. Give every amount a purpose before the month begins.

🐷

Save First

Set aside even a small amount when you receive money. A regular habit is more important than a perfect amount.

🛒

Pause Before Buying

Wait before impulse purchases and ask whether you need the item, can afford it, and have compared alternatives.

🔐

Protect Your Details

Never share passwords, one-time codes, or banking details. Be careful with links, offers, and requests for urgent payment.

📌

Plan for Study Costs

Keep a list of books, travel, supplies, and exam fees so important expenses do not arrive as surprises.

Before you spend

Ask: Is it a need or a want? Is this the best price? Will this affect an important goal? A short pause protects your future choices.
