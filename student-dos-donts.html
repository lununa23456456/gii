A Practical Student Compass

Small choices shape a strong student life.

Use these reminders to protect your time, health, relationships, money, and learning. Progress comes from repeatable choices, not from being perfect every day.

✅ Do this

  • Plan your top three tasks before starting the day.
  • Study actively by recalling, practising, and reviewing mistakes.
  • Sleep, eat, hydrate, and take movement breaks.
  • Ask questions when a concept is unclear.
  • Keep your promises and communicate respectfully.
  • Save some money and protect your passwords.
  • Ask for support when stress feels difficult to manage.

🚫 Avoid this

  • Do not wait until the last night for every assignment.
  • Do not compare your full journey with someone’s highlight reel.
  • Do not ignore persistent tiredness, sadness, or anxiety.
  • Do not spend money just because friends are spending.
  • Do not share private information or one-time security codes.
  • Do not let conflict grow when a calm conversation could help.
  • Do not treat one mistake as proof that you cannot improve.

Keep it realistic

Choose one or two reminders to practise this week. Sustainable habits grow slowly, and returning to your plan after a difficult day is part of progress.
