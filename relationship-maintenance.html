Healthy Connections

Build relationships with care, honesty, and boundaries.

Strong friendships and family connections need attention. Small acts of respect and clear communication help relationships stay supportive through busy student life.

Habits that keep connections healthy

👂

Listen Properly

Put distractions away, let the other person finish, and reflect back what you understood before giving advice.

💬

Speak Honestly

Use calm, direct words about what you need. Honest communication prevents small misunderstandings from becoming large ones.

🧱

Respect Boundaries

It is healthy to say no, ask for space, and protect your time. Respect other people’s boundaries too.

⏳

Make Time

Send a message, share a meal, or schedule a short call. Consistent attention matters more than expensive gestures.

🛠️

Repair Conflict

Talk when everyone is calmer, focus on the issue, apologise for your part, and agree on what can change next time.

🛡️

Choose Safety

Healthy relationships feel respectful and safe. Seek support if someone repeatedly controls, threatens, humiliates, or isolates you.

A useful question

After a conversation, ask: Did I listen, speak respectfully, and leave room for both people’s needs? This simple check can improve your relationships over time.
